## Monitoring plan

Thresholds other than PSI are **Assumptions** for this demo.

| metric | frequency | green | amber action | red action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Score PSI vs baseline | monthly | < 0.10 | 0.10-0.25: CSI to find the cause | > 0.25: model review |
| CSI per feature | monthly | < 0.10 | check data feed and mix | > 0.25: review that feature's bins |
| Approval rate at cutoff | monthly | within 3 pts of train (87%) | 3-6 pts: check PSI, CSI and policy | > 6 pts: credit committee |
| Calibration (predicted vs observed bad rate) | quarterly, once outcomes mature (2 yr) | ratio 0.8-1.25 | recalibrate master scale | redevelop |
| AUC / Gini on matured accounts | quarterly | Gini > 0.65 | investigate | Gini < 0.60: redevelop |
| Challenger gap (shadow LightGBM) | quarterly | <= 0.015 AUC | check what the challenger learned | > 0.03: redevelop |
| Input data: missing rate, 96/98 codes, out-of-range | every batch | as in training | fix feed | stop scoring |
